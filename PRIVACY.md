# Privacy Policy — Zetamac Stats Tracker

**Last updated: May 24, 2026**

Zetamac Stats Tracker is a Chrome extension that tracks your arithmetic
performance on arithmetic.zetamac.com.

## Data Collected

The extension collects the following data locally on your device:
- Your game scores
- Per-question solve times and arithmetic operation types
- Derived metadata (e.g., whether a problem required carrying/borrowing)

## How Data Is Used

This data is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage
(`chrome.storage.local`) and is used only to display your own historical
performance statistics within the extension. It is never transmitted,
shared, or uploaded anywhere.

## Third Parties

No data is shared with or sold to any third party.
